A homegrown brand, built on doing the basics properly.

Threadline (Private) Limited is a Karachi startup that designs everyday clothing for the city's climate and budgets. We work with two partner stitching units, sell online, on WhatsApp and from our studio-store in PECHS, and check every piece twice before it leaves us.

Our vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted homegrown everyday-wear brand by 2030, known for comfort, fair prices and honest service.

Our mission

To design comfortable, well-made everyday clothing suited to Karachi's climate and budget, deliver it quickly, and stand behind every piece we sell.

  • Comfort first. If it isn't comfortable in Karachi in June, we don't sell it.
  • Honest pricing. Clear prices, real discounts, no hidden charges.
  • Quality every time. Two quality checks on every order before dispatch.
  • Respect. For customers, colleagues, partners and tailors alike.

Dressing your team, or thanking your clients

Staff kurtas, polos and event tees with your logo, or year-end gift boxes. We share a sample before bulk production and send progress updates every Monday until delivery.

  1. Tell us what you need. A 20-minute call about quantity, sizes, design and deadline.
  2. Get a written quotation within 2 working days, valid for 15 days.
  3. Approve a sample before we stitch the full order.
  4. Receive on time, with a delivery note and invoice.
